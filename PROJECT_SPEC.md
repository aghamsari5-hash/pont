# PROJECT SPECIFICATION — PONT CAFE

Product:
PONT CAFE is a QR based digital menu.

Purpose:
Customers scan a QR code and quickly view cafe and restaurant menus.

Technology:
- Laravel
- PHP 8.3+
- MySQL/MariaDB
- Blade
- Tailwind CSS
- Vite

Customer flow:

Home:
Logo
Language switch
Two choices:
1. Cafe
2. Restaurant

Cafe:
- Hot Bar
- Cold Bar
- Dessert

Restaurant:
- Breakfast
- Lunch
- Dinner

Product card:
- One image
- Product name
- Price
- Availability status when needed

Not included:
- Online ordering
- Cart
- Payment
- Delivery
- Customer accounts
- Reviews
